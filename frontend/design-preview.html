FAQ

What is a credit?

A credit pays for one wallet check. Each check uses one credit.

Is this real money?

No. Credits are in test mode, so no real money moves.

Why pay privately with crypto?

Paying with crypto through a wallet such as Cake Wallet keeps your payment private.

क्या यह पैसा असली है?
